# Codex Account Switcher 1.8.5

Version 1.8.5 forecasts how long the pooled five-hour quota lasts across all saved accounts, so you can see whether the remaining pool will survive until the weekly reset.

## Pool usage pace forecast

- The app samples the remaining pool (sum of remaining percent across accounts) every 30 minutes and stores it in a local JSONL history with a 56-day retention, deduplicated per sampling bucket.
- The account panel gains a CodexBar-style utilization chart: capped bars over a full track, coloured by remaining level (green above 50%, orange above 10%, red at 10% or below), with date labels along the bottom.
- Hovering the chart shows the exact remaining value and timestamp for that sample.
- Replace raw pool/burn forecast text with an ordered Enough / Not Enough / Collecting verdict card.
- Add immediate Russian/English switching for the main panel, defaulting to Russian for new installations.
- Weekly reset timestamps captured from live usage snapshots are persisted with the history so the forecast compares EOL against the actual reset date.

The forecast needs roughly two days of history to become meaningful; it is marked `approx` until a week of samples has accumulated.

## Maintenance

- Infrastructure coverage now includes pool history load/save, retention pruning, forecast math, verdict evaluation, and the reset date roll-forward.

The release is ad-hoc signed and includes a SHA-256 checksum. It is not Apple-notarized, so macOS may show the standard warning for independently distributed software.
